## 1. Purpose and scope

The plugin helps prepare a report from Cowork activity and artifact metadata
available to the signed-in user. It can produce an HTML report and an Excel
workbook containing classifications, summaries, and modeled estimates. Its
availability and results depend on the user's access, source data, permissions,
and runtime configuration.

These terms describe use of the plugin itself. Use of Cowork, Microsoft 365,
Outlook, OneDrive, AI assistant or model providers, and other connected
services may be governed by separate terms, privacy notices, and
organizational controls.

not send email on its own and does not bypass the host's approval step.

Before approving a message, the user must verify the recipient, attachments,
and authority to share them. The organization is responsible for deciding
